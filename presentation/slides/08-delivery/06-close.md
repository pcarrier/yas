# The shell is still there. It is just not alone.

- Start a job, close the laptop, and pick it up from a phone.
- Open one remote window; its title, icon, input, clipboard, audio, and notifications come with it.
- Jump from local to `prod` without changing tools or teaching the browser another protocol.
- Let a browser, CLI, extension, and agent share live state without sharing one viewport.
